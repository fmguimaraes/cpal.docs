Everything the palette-changes doc claims holds on the surfaces it names: status text on background, card, secondary and on its own 12% soft fill passes in both themes (0 failures in the status block), chart-1 to chart-5 are at least 3:1 on background and card (chart-5 dark is exactly 3.00), primary-text on primary-soft over card is 4.74 (light) and passes in dark. The failures below are on things the palette doc does not cover: kit opacity modifiers, third-party (Leaflet) CSS, and token combinations on secondary, accent and muted surfaces.

## Method

1. **Build and serve.** `VITE_API_BASE_URL=http://localhost:4221/api/v1 vite build --outDir dist-e2e-4221`, `vite preview --port 4221`. The build is the production one (CSP meta tag in force).
2. **Browser run.** Playwright (Chromium, 1440x900), script `contrast.mjs` in the session scratchpad (not committed). Auth and API mocked with `page.route` like the E2E specs: account, 2 contracts, 12 devices with masses `null / 100 / 420 / 800` (so the four states offline, alert, warning, ok exist), history with 5 devices and 8 points each (3 Plotly charts, checklist), messages, OSM tiles replaced by a 1x1 PNG, client logo 404 (placeholder path). Routes: `/`, `/live-tracking` (contract 12 selected), `/history` (contract 12 selected), `/pilotage` (contract 12 selected), `/change-password`, `/login`, `/forgot-password`, `/reset-password?token=abc`, `/no-such-page`. Each in `dark` and `light` through `localStorage cpaltracker:theme`.
3. **Sampling.** For every visible element: computed `color` of each element with its own text, plus links (`a`), buttons, comboboxes, status badges (`data-slot=badge`), status dots (`span[aria-hidden].rounded-full`), icons inside buttons and links, input borders (1px control boundary), input text and `::placeholder`, Plotly series (`stroke`/`fill`), Plotly tick, legend and title text. Focus ring: 12 Tab stops per route and theme, `outline-color` and width of the focused element.
4. **Backgrounds.** The effective background is the stack of every ancestor `background-color` composited bottom-up (alpha aware, including `color-mix` results, parsed from `rgb()`, `color(srgb)` and `oklab()`), over the root background. This yields the real card, secondary, sidebar and soft-fill backgrounds (12% fills blend over the real card, not over the page). Text alpha and element opacity are composited over the background too. Elements over a map raster could not be resolved (tiles are mocked) and are noted.
5. **Ratios.** WCAG 2.1 relative luminance, `(L1+0.05)/(L2+0.05)`. Thresholds: 4.5 normal text; 3 large text (24 px, or 18.66 px bold), UI component boundaries and graphical objects (1.4.11), focus indicator. Disabled controls are exempt (1.4.3 inactive components) and listed separately.
6. **Token matrix.** Every color in `src/styles/tokens.css` parsed per theme and paired: all text roles on all seven surfaces, `*-foreground` on its own surface, filled status chips, every status color and primary over its 12% soft fill on background, card, secondary, sidebar (and popover for primary), control border and ring on the surfaces, status dots on surfaces, chart-1 to chart-5 on background and card. 350 pairs. Soft fill = `0.12*color + 0.88*surface` in sRGB, which is what `color-mix(in oklab, c 12%, transparent)` composites to.
7. **Excluded as artifacts.** The Plotly donut separator stroke (card colored, `surface` class) is not a data mark and is skipped. The Radix `Switch` has a transparent 1px border, so the script measured it as 1.00 (2 rows); the real boundary is the track fill, computed by hand in F5.
